# Terms of Service — Personal Assistant SMS

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

## What this is

A private, self-hosted personal assistant reachable by SMS, operated by an individual for
his own household. It is not offered to the public.

## Permitted use

Only the operator and family members he has explicitly added to the allowed-recipient list
may use this service. There is no public enrollment.

## Message frequency

Varies. Most messages are direct replies to a message a recipient sent. Some are automated
updates about the household's own property and equipment.

## Costs

Message and data rates may apply, according to the recipient's own mobile carrier plan.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop all messages. Reply **HELP** for help.

## No warranty

Provided as-is, with no warranty. Automated alerts are not a safety or emergency system
and must not be relied upon as one.

## Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
