# Privacy Policy — Personal Assistant SMS

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

This policy covers a private SMS service operated by an individual for his own household.
It is not a commercial service and is not available to the public.

## Who operates this

Robert Landry, an individual in the United States, operating a self-hosted personal
assistant system.

## Who receives messages

The operator and a small number of his immediate family members. Each recipient's mobile
number is added to the system's allowed-recipient list manually by the operator, only
after that person has given consent directly to him. A number cannot receive any message
until it has been added by hand. There is no public signup and no automated enrollment
path, so no one outside the household is able to subscribe.

## What is collected

The mobile numbers of permitted recipients, and the content of messages exchanged with
them. Messages are transmitted through Twilio, which processes them as the messaging
provider under its own privacy terms.

## How it is used

Solely to deliver replies to messages a recipient sends, and to send occasional automated
updates about the household's own property and equipment. This data is never sold, rented,
shared, or disclosed to any third party, and is never used for marketing.

## Retention

Message records are retained only as long as they are useful to the operator and may be
deleted at any time. Twilio retains delivery metadata under its own policies.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop all messages. Reply **HELP** for help.
